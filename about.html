Founder of Maternum Cura

Sumayyah Yaseen

Supporting families through birth and beyond

Sumayyah Yaseen is the founder of Maternum Cura, a doula practice serving Detroit and the surrounding Metro Detroit area. Her work supports families as they prepare for birth, go through labor, and settle in at home with a newborn.

Before birth, families can work with Maternum Cura on childbirth education and building a birth plan. During active labor, the practice offers in-person emotional and physical support. After birth, newborn care and practical help at home give families space to rest.

Maternum Cura offers a Medicaid package and custom private-pay packages. An introductory call gives families a chance to meet the practice, ask questions, and talk through the support they want.
