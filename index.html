Getting started

Two ways to work together.

Choose the path that fits how you plan to pay. Both begin with an introductory call.

Medicaid

For families who want to explore using Medicaid for prenatal doula support.

  1. Book an introductory call. Tell us what support you’re looking for.
  2. Review coverage and intake. We’ll explain how we verify eligibility and when to complete the separate Medicaid intake.
  3. Confirm next steps. We’ll explain how billing works for your plan before you begin.

Private pay

For families who plan to pay directly for their prenatal support package.

  1. Book an introductory call. Share your goals and ask about package options.
  2. Confirm your package. Agree on what’s included, the schedule, and the price.
  3. Pay through Stripe. Use the checkout link the practice sends after confirming your package.

The introductory call

Come as you are.

We’ll talk about where you are in pregnancy, what kind of support feels right, and any questions you want to ask. You’re welcome to invite a partner or support person.

We’ll also go over the next step for Medicaid intake or private-pay packages, depending on what works for your family.

Common questions

What’s included in prenatal support?

Prenatal support can include talking through birth preferences, practicing comfort measures, involving your support person, and planning for the early days at home. Ask on the introductory call which services are included in your package.

How does a doula work with my care team?

Your doula helps you prepare questions and offers practical and emotional support. Your medical care team handles exams, diagnoses, and treatment.

What if I’m unsure about Medicaid coverage?

Start with a Medicaid introductory call. The practice can explain what information is needed to check your plan and guide you through its intake and billing process.

When do I pay for a private-pay package?

After you and the practice confirm your package and price, you’ll receive a Stripe checkout link. You can ask about costs and payment timing during the introductory call.
