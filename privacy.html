Privacy Policy

Private-pay questionnaire

Your pregnancy or postpartum status and payment preference stay in the booking form in your browser. The website does not send those answers to Maternum Cura, the scheduling service, or the quote email.

Email, scheduling, and intake

The quote button opens an email draft with the services you selected. You can review the draft and decide whether to send it. Scheduling, Medicaid intake, and payments take place through separate services when available. Review their privacy information before entering personal details.

Sharing sensitive information

Please keep medical records, insurance ID numbers, and payment card details out of ordinary email. If we need sensitive information for your care or intake, call (313) 434-1456 and ask how to share it securely.

For general privacy questions, contact support@maternumcura.com.
